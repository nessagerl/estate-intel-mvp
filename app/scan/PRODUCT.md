# Estate Intelligence MVP

## The Problem

People who suddenly become responsible for an entire household often don't know what they have, what may be valuable, what deserves closer attention, how to sell it, or where to begin.

At the same time, estate-sale shoppers, collectors, dealers, and resellers spend significant time searching for the particular things they want.

## The Idea

Create a mobile-first application that helps people understand the things they own and eventually connects household assets with people interested in finding them.

## Three Ways to Enter

### 1. I Need Help With a Home

For someone settling an estate, downsizing, moving, or clearing a household.

The application guides them through the home room by room and helps identify what deserves attention and what their next steps could be.

### 2. I Love Finding Interesting Things

For estate-sale shoppers, collectors, dealers, and resellers.

Users tell us what kinds of things they are interested in so we can eventually show them relevant opportunities.

### 3. I'm Curious About Something I Own

A user photographs an item or collection.

The application helps identify it, explains what may be interesting about it, asks for additional photographs when necessary, and eventually allows the user to save it to "My Stuff."

## First MVP Goal

A stranger can open the website on a phone, understand what the product does, photograph one object, receive useful guidance, and want to continue.

## What Good AI Should Do

The AI should not pretend to know more than it does.

It should:

- identify likely categories and objects
- explain what visual clues it notices
- communicate uncertainty
- recognize potentially interesting features
- ask for better photographs when needed
- look for maker's marks, signatures, labels, model numbers, materials, and construction details
- recommend the next useful step
- distinguish identification from appraisal
- avoid giving false precision about value

The experience should feel like a knowledgeable guide helping someone investigate an object.

## We Are NOT Building Yet

- Auctions
- Payments
- Buyer-to-seller messaging
- Shipping
- Pickup scheduling
- Exact appraisals
- A nationwide marketplace
- Complex subscriptions

## Long-Term Vision

Over time, users can build a digital record of the things they own.

Collectors, dealers, resellers, and estate-sale shoppers can tell the platform what they are interested in finding.

When households eventually need to sell, downsize, move, or settle an estate, the platform can intelligently connect household assets with relevant demand.

The long-term asset is not simply an identification app.

It is a growing network of:

- household asset intelligence
- buyer demand intelligence
- market behavior
- disposition outcomes
- routing intelligence

## Core Principle

Build the smallest useful thing, put it in front of real people, learn from their behavior, and then decide what to build next.
